Common questions

How does Seen work?

Seen lets you send short, kind, anonymous messages that reach real people across the world, and receive uplifting messages back. There are no followers, no likes, and no pressure — just small moments of kindness.

Account & sign-in

Reporting & safety

Seen has zero tolerance for abusive, harmful, or objectionable content. We give you simple tools to stay in control:

  • Everything written is screened first — public posts, shared reflections and private replies all pass an automated safety review before they are published or delivered. If that review can’t be completed, the content is blocked rather than let through. Anything posted without a name attached is held to a stricter standard.
  • Report anything — long-press a message, or use Report or block on a shared reflection or a private reply, then choose a reason (Harmful, Spam, Inappropriate, or Other). Every report reaches a review queue; content that breaks our guidelines is removed for everyone.
  • Block someone — from the same menu, tap 🚫 Block to stop seeing that person entirely: their messages, their reflections, and any private replies from them. You can view and undo your blocks any time under Menu → Blocked accounts.

We aim to review reports and remove content that breaks our guidelines within 24 hours. For anything urgent, or to report a safety concern directly, email prince1489@googlemail.com.
