Delete Your Account

Seen · Account & data deletion

This page explains how to delete your Seen account and the data associated with it. You can delete your whole account yourself, inside the app, at any time — it takes effect straight away.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Seen on your phone, or go to www.seenapp.app, and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu (···) at the top right, then Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE, and tap Delete my account permanently.

Your account and your data are deleted immediately and can't be recovered. If you signed in with Apple on an iPhone or iPad, you'll be asked to confirm with Apple once — this also disconnects Seen from your Apple ID.

Once your account is deleted, this can't be undone. You can create a new account to use Seen again, but nothing from your old account comes back.

Delete individual content yourself

You don't need to delete your whole account to remove specific things:

  • Delete a message — press and hold one of your messages and choose Delete.
  • Delete a conversation — open a private chat, tap the menu (•••), and choose Delete chat.
  • Forget your conversation with Seen — open Seen in Messages, tap the menu (⋯), and choose Forget everything. This deletes the whole conversation, everything Seen has noticed, and any line you shared, straight away.

Can't sign in any more?

If you've lost access to your account and can't delete it in the app, email us at prince1489@googlemail.com from the email address on your Seen account, with the subject “Delete my Seen account”. We'll verify that the account is yours and delete it, normally within 30 days.
